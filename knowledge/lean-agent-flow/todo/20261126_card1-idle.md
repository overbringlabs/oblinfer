# Card 1 idle 88% of the token

`nsys` at 1k, committed tree: card 0 67.3% duty (40,992 launches, 5.4 us mean), card 1 **11.9%** (3,840
launches, 10.2 us mean, 0.82 ms of a 6.9 ms token). Card 1 is not slow — it runs its experts at 323 GB/s of its
427 ceiling — it is waiting.

Three options, in ascending cost:

1. **Pipeline the layers**: card 1 runs layer l's experts while card 0 runs layer l+1's dense work. Recovers card
   0's 33% idle. Purely a schedule change in `decoder.cu`, no kernel work.
2. **Let card 1 route for itself**: replicate `mlp.gate.weight` (1 MB/layer, 42 MB total) and delete four API round
   trips per layer, ~128 us/token, measured and described by agent B.
3. **Move dense work per layer to card 1** (per-layer device routing with a hop each, +40 hops/token). Balance would
   put both cards near 2.3 ms of work against card 0's 4.6, pointing at 250+ tok/s at 1k. The hop cost is the risk.

The symmetric pair halves the prize in option 1 and 3, because the slow-card penalty goes away; re-rank after the
retune rather than acting on today's numbers.
